Questions

Is anything here actually for sale?

No. This is a demo store used to generate realistic analytics data.

What gets tracked?

Page views and clicks, with the click position and the element clicked. The test console in the corner shows each event as it is sent.

How do I start a fresh session?

Open the test console and press "New session". Sessions also expire after 30 minutes of inactivity.

Where do I see the data?

Open the dashboard from the footer, or use "View in dashboard" in the test console to jump straight to your own journey.
